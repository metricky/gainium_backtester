# 007 — a percentage safety-order ladder accumulates tick rounding across levels

Bug spec (Part 0 / bug template). One defect, three copies of the same loop:

| copy | site | runs where |
|---|---|---|
| live bot engine | `main-app-sh` `src/bot/dcaHelper.ts` `createInitialDealOrders` | bot workers — the orders actually placed |
| dashboard preview | `main-dash-sh` `src/utils/bots/dca/example-orders-core.ts` `createDCAOrders` | the bot form's example orders |
| backtest engine | this repo, `src/helper/dcaBotFunctions.ts` `createOrders` | server-side and in-browser backtests |

## §1 Definition

A DCA bot with `dcaCondition: percentage` (or unset) and `scaleDcaType:
percentage` places safety order `i` at `step × Σ stepScale^k (k < i)` of the
start price beyond the start.

### §1.1 Every level lands within one tick of its configured price

- **Expected:** level `i` is `round(start × (1 ∓ step × Σ scale^k))`: at most
  half a tick off, whatever `i` is.
- **Actual:** level `i` is `round(level(i-1) ∓ start × step × scale^(i-1))`,
  so each level inherits the previous level's rounding. When the per-level
  distance is not a whole number of ticks, every level rounds the same way
  (round-half-up), so the error grows linearly with `i`. On a 0.001 tick at a
  price near 0.25, a 30 × 1% ladder ends 24.1–35.9% below the start instead
  of 30%.

### §1.2 Levels stay strictly separated

When the per-level distance is under one tick, consecutive levels must still
be at least one tick apart and never climb back past the previous level. That
is the existing behaviour: an equal price is pushed one tick further. It must
survive the §1.1 change, because a level rounded from the unrounded ladder
can land behind a previous level that was pushed.

### §1.3 Out of scope, unchanged

Indicator, custom-step and ATR/ADR-scaled ladders measure from the previous
order by design. Combo ladders (`comboHelper`, `createComboOrders`,
`comboBotFunctions`) carry the same arithmetic, but mini-grid ranges hang off
them, so they are tracked separately.

## §2 Evidence

- **Live state.** A paper DCA bot on Kraken, 30 × 1%, stepScale 1, 0G/USD
  (tick 0.001). Its open deal started at 0.254 and has resting safety orders
  at 0.251 / 0.248 / 0.245 / 0.242 / 0.239: three ticks apart for a
  2.54-tick step, so level 5 sits 5.9% down instead of 5%.
- **Executed, not transcribed.** The real `createInitialDealOrders` (main-app
  harness), `createDCAOrders` (vitest) and `createOrders` (this repo) all
  reproduce those five prices exactly from a 0.254 start, and agree with
  each other at every level of 11 configurations. The preview's last level
  at starts of 0.252 and 0.2512 is 35.71% and 35.86% below, the two values
  the reporter quoted.
- **Fine ticks.** On 1–8 decimal ticks the same drift is 6–14 ticks by level
  30. That is ≤0.01% of the price at BTC/ETH precision but 0.8% at
  `1.234e-05` / 1e-8.

## §3 Assumption (hypothesis, confirmed by §2)

The drift comes from stepping each level off the rounded previous price. A
test that pins every level within one tick of `start × (1 ∓ step × Σ
scale^k)` fails on the current code at level 3 of the first start price
(0.249), and only for that reason.
